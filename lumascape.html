01 / BUSINESS PROBLEM

Turning transaction data into business decisions

E-commerce businesses need reliable reporting to understand revenue growth, profitability, order activity, and product performance. Disconnected transaction records can make it difficult to evaluate these metrics consistently.

Financial Visibility

Track revenue, gross profit, gross margin, and year-over-year performance.

Product Performance

Identify the categories contributing most to sales and gross profit.

Management Reporting

Present consistent metrics and trends through an interactive dashboard.

05 / BUSINESS INSIGHTS

What the data revealed

Revenue and profit growth: Monthly revenue and gross profit increased substantially during the analysis period, especially in 2026.
Margin stability: Gross margin generally remained around 45%–46%, even as sales increased.
Category leadership: Transformers generated the highest net revenue and gross profit across the analyzed product categories.
Sales activity: Monthly orders and units sold increased over time, providing additional context for revenue growth.
Further investigation: Additional analysis is needed to separate the effects of order volume, pricing, product mix, and units per order.
