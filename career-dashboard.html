Comparing skill demand and advertised pay

This Excel project compares requested skills and advertised annual USD pay across data roles. It separates a prepared U.S. snapshot from an interactive Data Model view filtered to postings classified as exactly New York, NY.

Built in Excel desktop

Power Query

Jobs, JobSkills and SkillCategories load prepared CSVs into the Data Model.

Power Pivot & DAX

Relationships and measures support job counts, skill demand share, salary medians and salary coverage.

Charts & slicers

The role slicer controls skills only; the NYC slicer controls both interactive charts.

The original dashboard uses formulas, dropdowns and prepared aggregate tables. Its controls and refresh process are separate from the Data Model section. Cloud deployment is a future extension.

Skill pay comparison: The U.S. chart subtracts the selected role/location median from the median for eligible salary jobs mentioning each skill. Skills with fewer than 30 salary observations are excluded. Differences may reflect seniority, employers and overlapping skills; they do not show that learning a skill raises pay.

Interpretation and limitations

  • Separate scopes: The U.S. snapshot and exact-NYC view cover different populations and use different data pathways.
  • Mixed seniority: Role groups come from job-title text and combine postings across seniority levels.
  • Incomplete salaries: Pay represents eligible advertised annual USD range midpoints, rather than actual compensation.
  • Small sample: NYC BI Analyst median pay of $165,000 is based on five jobs and is not a reliable general finding.
  • Skill mentions: These measure employer requests, not candidate proficiency or causal effects on pay.

Validation and refresh

Session validation on October 17, 2026 reported a successful Refresh All with no errors and expected row counts for all three queries. Switching the role slicer to Data Engineer updated skills while the salary chart retained all five role groups. Data Analyst / NYC TRUE was restored.

Data Model refresh uses Data → Refresh All and requires valid local source paths. The prepared snapshot requires its separate preparation/rebuild process. Revised snapshot skill-pay formulas and dynamic titles were not independently audited.
